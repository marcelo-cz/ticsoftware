2025: The Year to Transform Your HPE Nonstop Platform

Unlock the full potential of your Nonstop systems

December 16, 2024

Your Nonstop platform has been the backbone of your business. But in 2025, you may be wrestling with critical questions: Should you maintain current systems? Invest in modernization? Or explore alternatives?

The reality: HPE Nonstop remains a robust, valuable asset with unparalleled total cost of ownership. However, the challenges are real: aging support staff, developer recruitment concerns, and pressure to justify continued investment.

The solution: Address key misconceptions and unlock proven strategies to future-proof your platform.

❌ Common Misconceptions

Misconception #1: "Platform is outdated due to green screen interfaces"

The reality: Nonstop fully supports modern tools to enable GUI and browser UIs.

Misconception #2: "COBOL limits development capabilities"

The reality: COBOL isn't the issue—lack of documentation is. Any developer can work with business logic when proper documentation exists.

Misconception #3: "Difficulty in attracting new talent"

The reality: Nonstop is complex but learnable. Better education and training programs solve this challenge.

Misconception #4: "Established programs are too complex to understand"

The reality: The solution is not abandonment but attention—comprehensive documentation and knowledge preservation.

✅ Solutions & Realities

Modern Capabilities

Nonstop is far more versatile than many realize. Your platform now supports:

  • Web applications
  • RESTful APIs
  • Kafka integration
  • Java and modern languages
  • GitHub DevOps integration

Benefit: Get the best of both worlds—Nonstop reliability + modern technology flexibility.

Focus on Business Logic

The critical aspect is understanding business logic, not just programming language. Invest in:

  • Comprehensive documentation of existing code
  • Codebase analysis and mapping
  • Knowledge base creation

Result: Any developer can understand and maintain your systems.

Education & Training

Build expertise across all levels:

  • C-Suite: Understand platform value proposition
  • IT Management: Know what needs modernization
  • Developers: Master Nonstop capabilities + modern tools

The Bottom Line

Your Nonstop environment isn't holding you back—it's a competitive advantage. With the right attention, tools, and strategy, it becomes your innovation platform.

📋 Your 2025 Action Plan

  • Invest in Documentation: Use modern tools to create comprehensive documentation for established systems
  • Embrace Modernization: Leverage Nonstop's support for modern technologies to keep systems current
  • Prioritize Training: Implement robust training programs to build and maintain expertise
  • Leverage Expert Support: Partner with experienced organizations to access cutting-edge tools and services
