◆ Messaging

Deliver Nonstop reports anywhere, automatically.

TIC's Messaging products integrate Nonstop data with other platforms through format conversion and data delivery. TeleMail sends reports and files by email; TeleFTP transmits them to remote FTP servers. Both run on your Nonstop system, with no external hardware or software.

The Tele family

Two ways to get Nonstop data where it needs to go

Automated, reliable delivery of reports and files, so critical information reaches the right people at the right time.

Email delivery

TeleMail

TeleMail delivers Nonstop spooler reports and files via the Internet to virtually anywhere, as an email message or an attachment.

  • Sends reports and documents in the message itself or as attachments
  • Converts output to PDF, HTML or ZIP files automatically
  • Distribution lists and detailed log files for status and statistical reporting
  • Doubles as a proactive monitoring and alert system for system anomalies
  • Secure option for SSL and TLS SMTP

File transfer

TeleFTP

TeleFTP monitors multiple spooler locations and automatically transmits Nonstop files and reports to remote FTP servers. Built on TIC's Tele2000 architecture, it does much more than an FTP client.

  • Watches multiple spooler locations and sends reports as they arrive
  • Converts data to TXT, PDF, HTML or ZIP before transmission
  • Detailed error logging and configurable retries for error-free transfers
  • GUI installer and management console to monitor FTP activity and process status
  • Extensible through the Tele2000 API; scalable to your throughput needs
