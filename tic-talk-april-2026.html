TIC Navigator

What is TIC Navigator?

TIC Navigator is an AI-powered intelligence platform that unlocks the business value hidden inside HPE NonStop applications. It automatically analyzes NonStop source code—COBOL, SCOBOL, TAL/pTAL, C/C++, and TACL—and transforms complex, decades-old systems into clear, searchable insights.

Explore Navigator →

Key Features

  • Automatic Code Analysis: Intelligently parses and understands NonStop source code in multiple languages
  • Business Rules Extraction: Identifies and documents business logic and workflows
  • Searchable Documentation: Creates comprehensive, searchable documentation of your systems
  • AI-Powered Insights: Leverages machine learning to provide actionable recommendations
  • Modernization Roadmap: Helps identify opportunities for system improvement and modernization

Why TIC Navigator?

Understanding legacy NonStop systems is critical for maintenance, compliance, and modernization initiatives. TIC Navigator makes it easier than ever to:

  • Onboard new team members faster
  • Ensure compliance with business rules
  • Plan modernization strategies
  • Reduce operational risk
  • Maximize the value of your NonStop investments
